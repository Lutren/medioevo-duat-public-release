# Commercial Audit Report

Date: 2026-05-12

## Positioning

MEDIOEVO / DUAT should be positioned as a public-safe body of work for local-first agent orchestration, handoffs, visual systems, books, templates and audit frameworks.

## ICP

- Technical creators preparing a public release from a complex private workspace.
- Indie operators using AI agents across long-running projects.
- Small teams that need evidence, handoffs and claim boundaries before publication.
- Readers interested in MEDIOEVO books once approved links exist.

## Commercial Risk

- Overclaiming intelligence, prediction or scientific validation.
- Confusing private canon with public product material.
- Showing products without exact links or readiness.
- Using external scripts or API keys where simple links are enough.
- Selling books or asset packs before approved public sources exist.

## Recommended Publication Sequence

1. Stabilize About, Store and Gumroad direct link.
2. Collect approved book titles and purchase links.
3. Package Agent Handoff Kit with synthetic examples.
4. Publish Observacionismo Audit Pack only as a public-safe method.
5. Offer custom audit service manually after scope review.
