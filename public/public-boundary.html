Public-safe release boundary

What is public and what remains protected

MEDIOEVO publishes open foundations while protecting the worlds, private systems and raw research that should not be exposed.

Public

  • MIT tools and public-safe SDKs.
  • ActionGate, WitnessLog, handoff and release-checklist patterns.
  • Synthetic demos, falsifiers and sanitized research workflows.
  • Public explanations of Observacionismo and OSIT as proposed theory and engineering method.
  • Curated templates, briefings and implementation notes.

Protected

  • Unpublished books, full canon and editorial source vaults.
  • RPG/TCG mechanics, lore, assets and bridge code.
  • Complete DUAT/GEODIA, Wabi-Sabi internals, private runtime and OS internals.
  • Raw prompts, real datasets, account state, credentials, secrets and local runtime logs.
  • Proprietary calibration, complete formulas and raw research dumps.

Non-claims

MEDIOEVO is not presented as achieved AGI, solved consciousness, validated physics, clinical diagnosis or guaranteed safety. The public promise is evidence before action, state-aware processing, traceable agent work, open foundations and protected worlds.
