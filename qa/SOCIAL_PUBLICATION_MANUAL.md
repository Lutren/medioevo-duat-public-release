## Pre-Publication Checklist

- Open [https://medioevo.space](https://medioevo.space).
- Open the GitHub release.
- Review the suggested image.
- Confirm the post contains no secrets, private canon, unpublished research, local logs, or internal paths.
- Avoid exaggerated claims, externally validated AGI claims, government-production claims, or production claims without evidence.
- Publish manually only after operator approval.
