OSIT and the Tripartite Information Framework

This page frames OSIT as an operational method and formal-lab research program. It is not presented as proof of a universal physical law.

OSIT

OSIT treats observation as state-dependent. An agent does not interpret a task from zero; it interprets from current residue, available evidence, constraints and gates. The practical loop is simple: observe, classify, verify, act, witness, hand off.

Three information layers

Formal layer: hypotheses, metrics, falsifiers and simulation-friendly operators.

Operational layer: local runtime, gates, rollback, task packets, WitnessLog and session fingerprints.

Public layer: summaries, roadmap and evidence boundaries that can be shared without exposing private execution.

ScienceClaimGate

Public writing separates proposed theory from measured software behavior. DUAT and GEODIA summaries use synthetic simulation language and do not claim real-world prediction.
