## Boundary

These examples are synthetic and public-safe. They do not include private
MEDIOEVO books, Fragmentos, RPG/TCG material, Claudio/Wabi/DUAT private runtime,
raw prompts, credentials, local agent messages, internal workpacks or real
datasets.

They do not claim deployment prediction, universal-science proof, AGI or
fairness guarantees. The correct posture is operational methodology, examples,
tests and explicit gates.
